How do I apply for GTD access?+

Follow @LineZero_NFT, like and repost the pinned post, then submit your X username and public EVM wallet address. Your tasks are verified directly with X, one step at a time.

Does applying guarantee my place?+

Your application joins the review list. Social tasks are checked through your connected X account. The team announces the final GTD list on X.

Do I need to connect a wallet?+

No. Paste only your public EVM address. Applying is free and does not ask for a signature, seed phrase or transaction.

Where will mint details be announced?+

Follow the official @LineZero_NFT account for the mint date, final allowlist and collection updates.
